How to Play

1. The Prompt: A funny question appears, like "Who's the worst driver?"

2. Predict: Quick! Guess who the group will pick as #1 for bonus points.

3. Rank: Secretly rank everyone from most likely (#1) to least likely.

4. Reveal: The group's consensus is revealed!

5. Score: Match the group's consensus to earn points. Most points wins!
